Croak & Dagger

100 Frogs Must Fall

The pond is overrun. 100 frogs, every one dressed for the occasion (emo, cowboy, wizard, disco, astronaut and many more), stand between you and peace and quiet. Build a deck of slapstick scenario cards, control the weather and turn the pond against them.

Runs in your browser. Saves on this device automatically.

What's Inside

69 Slapstick Cards

Frying Pan Bonk, Rubber Duck Decoy, Disco Ball, Anvil Drop, Melt It With a Laser, Crush It With a Giant Foot…

Weather & Combos

Rain, drought, snow, fog and night all change the fight. Soak a frog, then strike it with lightning for triple damage.

100 Talking Frogs

Each frog has its own outfit, animations, quirk and one-liners. There's a boss every 10 levels and a Reaper at the end.

Make It Yours

Earn coins, unlock charms, upgrade cards, dress your hero and decorate your own house with trophies.

Keep Playing

Daily challenge, endless mode, achievements, a codex, a bestiary, stats and three difficulty levels.

Chiptune Soul

Music and sound effects are made live in your browser. Press M to mute anytime.
