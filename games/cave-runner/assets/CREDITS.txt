Cave Runner - asset credits and licences
========================================

3D models in assets/models/*.glb were built by Lost Aura Games with Blender 4.2 (headless, Python scripts).
Every texture is procedurally generated and baked in Blender (base colour, tangent-space normal map,
ambient occlusion / roughness / metalness packed as ORM, emissive). No photo textures or paid assets are used.
