3. What we collect and why

a) In your browser (localStorage)

Our pages and games use your browser's localStorage, not tracking cookies, to remember game saves and settings, your language (lag.lang), a random MEOWTEOR leaderboard player ID, Mythweald characters and settings (mythweald.chars.v1, mythweald.opt), and, if you sign in, your login session (lag.auth.v1). This stays on your device; you can delete it by clearing your browser's site data. Signing out removes the session.

b) MEOWTEOR leaderboard (optional)

If you choose a player name, we store that name, your best score, a random player ID made by your browser, and timestamps. Names and scores are shown publicly on the leaderboard. Purpose: running the leaderboard you asked to join.

c) Accounts (optional, for Mythweald online play)

An account is only needed to play Mythweald online. If you create one, we store your email address, a securely hashed password (we cannot see your password), an account ID, sign-up and sign-in times, your confirmation that you are {{MIN_AGE}} or older and agree to the Terms, and security logs kept by our authentication provider (such as IP address and browser type). Purpose: providing your account, keeping it secure, and sending service emails such as password resets.

d) Mythweald online play (closed testing, optional)

Mythweald can be played as a guest, with characters saved only in your browser. If you sign in and play an online character, we store, with our cloud database provider:

  • Your character: its name (shown publicly to other players), class, level, save data (quests, skills, position and settings), gold, and the equipment and bag items it owns, each with a random item ID.
  • Trading and market data: trades you make (both character names, the items and gold offered, and the result), your Unity of the Trade listings (your character name, the item, the price, status and dates; active listings are visible to every signed-in player), purchases, the 1% market tax, mail (sale proceeds, bought items, returned listings), and a ledger of these economy events with dates, kept to prevent duplication, cheating and fraud.
  • Location checks: the map region and coordinates your game reports from time to time, so the server can check that trades and market use happen in range and in a hub town.
  • Chat: whispers (private messages: sender, recipient, text and time) and global chat messages (character name, text and time). Global chat is shown to every player who is online. Party chat, party combat updates and player positions are sent live to the players in the same party or region and are not stored by us.
  • Your block list: the players you block. Blocked players cannot whisper you or trade with you, and their chat is hidden for you.

Chat safety: profanity and slurs are automatically masked (shown as #%$) in chat, and character names that contain them are refused. Whispers and global chat are filtered and rate-limited on our server; party chat is filtered in the game. This is automatic filtering, not live moderation, so it can miss things; if you report a player to us, we may read the relevant stored messages to act on the report.

Closed testing: Mythweald is in closed testing. Characters, levels, gold, items, listings and mail may be reset or deleted at any time while we develop the game. Purpose of this processing: running the online features you choose to use (characters, trading, market, mail, parties and chat), keeping them fair and safe, and preventing cheating and abuse.

e) MEOWTEOR NFT skins and wallets (optional)

Some MEOWTEOR skins (Pro Items from top-100 leaderboard events and event skins such as the Halloween Black Widow) can be claimed as ERC-1155 NFTs ("MEOWTEOR Pro Items", contract 0x7dde94d0299189ff7b6b092e788d382edb54b94c) on the Immutable zkEVM blockchain. This is optional; you never need a wallet to play. If you link a wallet (MetaMask or Immutable Passport), we store your player ID, your linked public wallet address, and records of the skins you earned, claimed and minted (with dates and transaction details). We pay the minting fee. Purpose: delivering the NFT you claimed, showing the matching skin in game to whoever holds it, and preventing duplicate claims and fraud. We never ask for or store your private keys or seed phrase.

Sanctions screening: to comply with sanctions laws, when you link or use a wallet we check its address against OFAC sanctions lists, using the Chainalysis sanctions oracle, and we look up the country of your IP address using DB-IP to block claims and trading from embargoed regions. Your IP address is used for this check and is not kept as part of your profile. Wallets or regions that fail screening cannot claim or trade NFTs.

Blockchains are public and permanent: wallet addresses, NFT ownership and transfers, including mints and trades on our NFT Market or other Immutable marketplaces, are recorded on a public blockchain that anyone can view. We cannot change or delete blockchain records, even if you delete your account; we can only delete the data we store on our own servers. Immutable and the marketplaces you use handle data under their own privacy policies.
