Mythweald

The Hollow Crown

A top-down mythological online RPG. Wake in Hearthmoor with a strange ember-mark on your palm, then quest, fight and trade your way across a huge world of enchanted forests, burned lands, frozen peaks, deserts and sunken ruins, alongside other players.

Closed Testing Tester login

Closed Testing. Mythweald is currently playable only by invited testers. It is still being built, so progress may be reset and things may change, including balance, items and features.

Runs in your browser on desktop and phone. Testers sign in with their Lost Aura account to play.

What's Inside

4 Classes

Play a Knight, Thief, Mage or Summoner. Each class has its own abilities, skill tree and gear, plus a gathering interest for when you're not questing.

A Huge World

Travel from the Whisperwood through kingdoms, burned lands, frozen mountains, deserts, sunken ruins and the shadowlands, with a story questline the whole way.

Dungeons from Level 20

Instanced dungeons open from level 20, full of elites, bosses and rare loot. Bring your party and the dungeon gets tougher to match.

Up to Level 200

Grow your hero all the way to level 200 with skills, crafting and ever stronger equipment.

Trading & the Unity of the Trade

Trade gear and gold with nearby players, or list items on the Unity of the Trade, a live market in the five hub towns. Sales and purchases arrive by mail.

Parties & Chat

Team up in parties of up to 4 and fight the same monsters together. Talk to everyone in global chat, to your party, or whisper a single player.

Good to Know

  • Free. No ads, no tracking and no in-game purchases.
  • During closed testing, only approved tester accounts can play. Sign in with your Lost Aura Games account: your online character, gold and gear are kept on our server.
  • Trading, the market, mail, parties, whispers and global chat need a signed-in online character.
  • Chat is filtered: profanity and slurs are masked, and player names are checked too. Type /block NAME to block a player.
  • Closed testing: progress may be reset and things may change. Found a bug or a player breaking the rules? Email lostauragames@gmail.com.
  • Best with a keyboard and mouse. On phones, turn your device sideways.
