A Space Cat Tap-Flyer

One cat. One button. Infinite space. Tap to make quick, sharp hops through the gaps in an endless asteroid belt. Every gap is +1, every run gets faster and tighter, and there is no finish line. Just you, your helmet and the next gap. How far can your cat fly?

What's Inside

One-Tap Flying

Tap, click or press Space for a quick, snappy hop. Easy to learn, hard to master, and every run takes just seconds to start.

Endless & Ramping

+1 for every gap. The gaps get tighter, faster and start to move, and the difficulty keeps climbing forever.

6 Zones

Fly through the Asteroid Belt, Satellite Spires, Crystal Caverns, Laser Gates, Candy Nebula and The Void. The scenery changes every 25 points.

51 Cosmetics

Furs, space suits, helmets and hats, face gear like shades and monocles, plus capes, wings and jetpacks. Crown your cat or make it a space cowboy.

17 Trails

Leave stardust, rainbows, fire, pixel hearts, chiptune notes, glitches and more behind you as you fly.

Stars, Medals & Ranks

Earn stars every run to spend in the Wardrobe, win medals from bronze all the way to cosmic, and climb the live global top 100.
