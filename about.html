About

A small arcade with a lost glow

Lost light, found in pixels. Lost Aura Games makes retro-styled browser games. They're quick to start, easy to learn and full of little secrets.

▶ Press Play

Every game runs right here in your browser. You don't need to download, install or sign up for anything.

★ Pixel Made

Art and sound are crafted pixel by pixel and note by note, inspired by classic consoles and arcade cabinets.

⚑ Saves Stay With You

Progress is saved in your browser on your device. Some games let you export a save code to move it somewhere else.

☉ Any Screen

Works with a mouse, touch or keyboard, on a big monitor or a small phone (landscape is best for phones).

Privacy

This site has no ads, analytics or trackers. Game saves stay in your own browser's local storage and are never uploaded. The one exception is MEOWTEOR's global leaderboard: if you choose a player name, that name and your best score are sent to the leaderboard server, along with a random player ID your browser makes up so it can find your row.

Now Playing

Croak & Dagger is a slapstick deckbuilder where you battle 100 frogs in outfits. Orbit Hills is a pixel-art hill-climb racer across 10 worlds. MEOWTEOR is a space cat tap-flyer with a global leaderboard. More games are on the way.
