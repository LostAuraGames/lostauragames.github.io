About

A small arcade with a lost glow

Lost light, found in pixels. Lost Aura Games makes retro-styled browser games. They're quick to start, easy to learn and full of little secrets.

▶ Press Play

Every game runs right here in your browser. You don't need to download, install or sign up for anything.

★ Pixel Made

Art and sound are crafted pixel by pixel and note by note, inspired by classic consoles and arcade cabinets.

⚑ Saves Stay With You

Progress is saved in your browser on your device. Some games let you export a save code to move it somewhere else.

☉ Any Screen

Works with a mouse, touch or keyboard, on a big monitor or a small phone (landscape is best for phones).

Privacy

This site has no ads, analytics or trackers. Game saves stay in your own browser's local storage. Only two optional things are stored on our server (Supabase): MEOWTEOR's global leaderboard, if you choose a player name (that name, your best score and a random player ID), and a player account, which you only need to buy coins (your email, coin balances and purchase history). Payments are handled by our reseller, the merchant of record. Read the Privacy Policy.

The Mythweald closed test adds a third: if you sign in to play it online, your character, gold, gear, trades, market listings, mail, chat messages and block list are stored with our cloud database provider. Guest play stays on your device.

Now Playing

MEOWTEOR is a space cat tap-flyer with a global leaderboard. Orbit Hills is a pixel-art hill-climb racer across 10 worlds. Croak & Dagger is a slapstick deckbuilder where you battle 100 frogs in outfits. More games are on the way.

Mythweald is a top-down mythological MMO with 4 classes, dungeons, trading and parties, now in closed testing.
