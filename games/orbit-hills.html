Orbit Hills

A Galaxy Climb Racer

Two pedals, ten worlds and one rule: land on your wheels. Hold GAS to climb, feather BRAKE to stay upright and launch off every crest. The hills change every run, the gravity changes every world, and every credit you earn makes your ride faster.

▶ Play now

Runs in your browser on desktop and phone. Saves on this device automatically.

What's Inside

10 Worlds

Start on Hearth, then drive across low-gravity Lunaris, Dune Nebula, Frostbite Moon, Cinder Prime, Neon Grid and more, all the way to the Event Horizon.

Real Gravity, Real Flips

Every world has its own gravity and grip. Catch air, pull backflips and frontflips for bonus credits, but land on your roof and the run is over.

New Hills Every Run

Tracks are generated fresh each time you drive. They get steeper, slicker and gappier the further you go.

Upgrade Everything

Spend credits on engine, fuel tank, suspension, tires and air control. Then save up for new worlds.

7 Cars & Cosmetics

From the Pixel Buggy to the Rocket Car and Comet Bike. Pick paint jobs, decals and toppers to make each one yours.

Chiptune Engine

Music and engine sounds are made live in your browser, with fuel cans and coins to grab along the way.

Controls

GasHold → or D, or the green GAS pedal (bottom right on touch)
Brake / reverseHold ← or A, or the red BRAKE pedal (bottom left on touch)
In the airGas tilts the nose up (backflip), brake tips it down (frontflip)
PauseEsc / P or the pause button
RetryEnter / R on the results screen
TouchBoth pedals work with multi-touch, so you can hold one and tap the other

Good to Know

  • Free. No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • Progress saves in this browser. Use Settings → Export to back up your save or move it to another device.
  • On phones, turn your device sideways for the best view.
  • Watch your fuel. Grab the red canisters to refill, and keep momentum on slick ground.
